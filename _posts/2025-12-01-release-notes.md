## Bug fixes

### Classic Pipelines

#### SSO configuration drawer lost your input when you clicked outside it

When you configured a new single sign-on (SSO) provider in **Account Settings → Single Sign-On**, clicking anywhere outside the configuration drawer closed it immediately. All the values you had entered were discarded, and you had to fill in the whole configuration again.

This is now fixed.

For details on configuring SSO, see [Single Sign-On]({{site.baseurl}}/docs/administration/single-sign-on/).
